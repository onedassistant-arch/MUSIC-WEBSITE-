Nurturing Musical Aspirations Since 1998

Established in R.S.Puram, Coimbatore, **Krishnanjali Musical Training Centre** stands as a beacon of academic excellence in fine arts. Over the past 26 years, we have successfully nurtured thousands of students, guiding them from fundamentals to prestigious stage recitals.

We combine authentic traditional instructions with global curricula, preparing learners for recognized graded examinations through **Trinity College London** (for Western instruments) and **Bridge Academy Chennai** (for classical art forms).

Academy Discipline

Rules & Regulations

Discipline is the foundation of artistic excellence. Kindly review our academy protocols.

80% Minimum Attendance

Consistent learning is vital. Every student must maintain at least 80% monthly class attendance.

No Compensations

Missed weekend sessions cannot be compensated with special weekdays makeup hours. Plan wisely.

12 Months Fee Scale

Fees are calculated and due for all 12 calendar months, irrespective of personal leaves or public holidays.

Late Fee Regulation

A standard late fine of ₹100 per week is applied for monthly dues paid past the scheduled due date.

Enrollment Documents

Submission of Aadhaar photocopy and two passport-sized color photos is mandatory upon final admission.

Annual Concert Contribution

A compulsory contribution of ₹2,000 is required annually for stage arrangements and concert rentals.
